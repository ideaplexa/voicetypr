# Benchmark clips

Short real-speech clips used only for accuracy/latency benchmarks.

- `ls-*.flac` — LibriSpeech ASR corpus (test set), Panayotov et al., OpenSLR 12,
  licensed CC BY 4.0: https://www.openslr.org/12
- `mls-de-*.opus`, `mls-es-*.opus` — Multilingual LibriSpeech (MLS), Pratap et
  al., OpenSLR 94, licensed CC BY 4.0: https://www.openslr.org/94

References in `manifest.json` are the corpus transcripts. Audio is unmodified.
