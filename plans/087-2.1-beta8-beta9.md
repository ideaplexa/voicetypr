## 4. "Only failed" audio saving (beta.8 item 1)

Today: with "Don't save", a failed take is kept in memory for 10 minutes (30 s for no speech),
at most 3, and is lost when the app quits (`recording/kept.rs`, `RecoveryKind::ttl_ms`).

Contract:
- Recording audio setting gets three choices: **Don't save**, **Only failed** (new), and keep for
  N days / forever.
- **Default "Only failed" for everyone**, including current "Don't save" users (founder decision).
  Migration follows invariant 9: Rust settings and `src/types.ts` together; existing
  `save_recordings: false` becomes Only failed once; users who pick "Don't save" afterwards stay
  there. Show the change once in What's new.
- Only failed keeps a failed take on disk until it is retried successfully, discarded, or
  7 days pass; it survives restarts and appears in Home "Needs you". A successful retry deletes
  the audio. Nothing else is kept.
- "Don't save" keeps today's short in-memory window so Retry still works for 10 minutes.
- Keep the existing protected-path and generation guards; no audio, path or transcript in logs.
- Needs adversarial review (data loss, storage, migration) and a real-Mac check: fail, quit,
  relaunch, retry from Home; retry-fails card; Delete audio removes the file.
