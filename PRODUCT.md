# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

A desktop app (Tauri webview UI) on macOS 14+ and Windows 10+. It must respect each OS's native window chrome, menu bar or tray, and keyboard conventions.

## Users

People who write a lot at a computer and would rather talk than type:
- professionals in pain or short on time, and slow or tired typists
- developers and founders who dictate prompts, messages and notes
- multilingual users (including Bangla, Hindi, Vietnamese and Chinese)

Windows users are a key, underserved audience; their alternatives are Dragon or Win+H. Most users are mainstream, not tech enthusiasts.

## Product Purpose

Press a hotkey, speak, and the text appears at the cursor in any app. It is fast and accurate, and it "just works" on both macOS and Windows. Success means people dictate daily without thinking about the tool, and never lose words.

## Positioning

- Local and offline AI first, with cloud engines (bring your own key) and LAN sharing as choices.
- The recording island never steals focus.
- A kept recording is never lost.
- Optional Polish never answers or rewrites what you said.
- Equal care on Windows and macOS.
- Lifetime license, no subscription required.

## Operating Context

- It lives in the menu bar or tray and on a hotkey. The main window is opened occasionally, for setup, history, insights and settings.
- Dictation happens inside other apps (editors, chat, mail, browsers, terminals). The island at the screen edge is the only surface during a dictation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Engines:
  - local: Parakeet (macOS only, for now) and Whisper
  - cloud: Soniox, Deepgram, OpenAI, Groq and Cohere
  - another Voicetypr on the LAN
- Polish via AI providers, Claude Code or Codex.
- History, Insights, Dictionary (words, corrections, snippets) and file transcription.
- Privacy: no transcript, audio, clipboard, prompt, key, path or window title in logs or analytics. One consent switch, pre-ticked with clear copy.
- License copy: lifetime, so never "expired" or "renew". Only trial-ended and Recheck states.
- Never say "Whisper" in marketing copy; say local or offline AI.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is spelled "Voicetypr".
- Existing logo mark.
- Voice: plain, short, human; no jargon. Errors say what happened and what to do.

## Product Principles

1. Simple choices over modes. Fewer pages, fewer settings, sensible defaults; power features live under Settings → Advanced.
2. Never lose words, and never steal focus.
3. Every surface earns its place. Don't repeat the same information across screens.
4. Equal quality on macOS and Windows, using native conventions on each.
5. Honest status: show what's happening and what needs the user, nothing more.
