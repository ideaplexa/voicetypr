Motion rules shared by every direction

Honest level. The meter only moves with real sound. A flat meter means the mic is really quiet, so it doubles as a mic check.

Fast attack, slow release. Levels rise in about 30 ms and fall over about 180 ms. That's what makes a meter feel alive instead of jittery.

Springs you can interrupt. Every size change chases one target on a spring, so a new dictation mid-animation just retargets. Nothing queues.

One accent, with meaning. Sage means done, amber means it needs you. Working states use motion, not colour, so "still busy" never looks like "act now".

Still when it should be. Nothing loops while ready, pasted or needing you, apart from a brief first pulse. Motion only while there's something happening.

Where this comes from. Nothing's dot-matrix Glyph UI, Teenage Engineering's one-accent screens, and the interaction craft of Emil Kowalski and Rauno Freiberg.
