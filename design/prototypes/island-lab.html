What makes it feel right

One shape, one spring. Width, height and corners chase one target on a spring (response 0.42 s, bounce 0.2). A new state mid-motion just redirects it; nothing queues or jumps.

Content choreography. Outgoing content fades in 80 ms. Incoming content waits 60 ms, then lifts 4 px and sharpens from a slight blur over 200 ms.

Shared elements. The app icon stays on screen in every state. The wave becomes the progress arc, and the arc closes into the check, so your eye follows one thing.

Weight. The island dips 2% for a beat before it opens wide, so it feels like it has mass.

Feedback stack. Newest in front, older ones peek behind (3 at most, then "+N"). Timed items leave in arrival order. Blocking problems stay until fixed. It tucks away while you dictate.

Honest context. App, style, mic and engine are read at key press, and the island shows what will happen. Only the app name and icon are shown, never window titles.
